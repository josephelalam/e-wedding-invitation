0 · Rules of the day

  • Priority order if time runs out: Cast refs → Reels 2, 3, 6 → Reels 1, 4, 5 → Ad bank → Launch kit → B-roll.
  • Only use models wearing the ∞ Unlimited badge today. Anything else silently burns your 10 remaining credits.
  • Workflow per shot: generate the keyframe still first (Nano Banana Pro, attaching the character reference images for everyone named in the shot), then animate that image with the motion prompt in an unlimited image-to-video model. Render 5–10 takes per shot, keep the best 2 — that's what unlimited buys you.
  • Aspect: 9:16 vertical for all video and story assets; 1:1 for grid statics. Set it in the UI every time.
  • Video model lineup (all ∞ on your trial): people & emotion → Kling 2.5 (5s max) · longer or sweeping shots → Veo 3.1 Fast (8s max, no audio — fine, music is added in edit) · complex crowd scenes (zaffe, dabke) → Sora 2 · talking lines → Higgsfield Speak · simple camera-move b-roll → Higgsfield DoP. When a shot looks off after 3 takes, switch model before rewording.
  • Download discipline: name files r2-sA-t03.mp4 (reel–shot–take). Rejected takes go in a broll-extra folder — never delete.
  • No AI Arabic text, ever. Video models butcher Arabic script. All عربي text (hooks, names, logo) gets added tomorrow in editing with your own fonts. Prompts below keep phone screens as unreadable glow for the same reason.
  • Green boxes = screen-recording slots — real captures of the live product, recorded separately (tomorrow is fine). Don't generate these.

In Soul 2.0 (∞ Unlimited on your trial), generate 5 stills per character — keep the base description identical every time, vary only the framing line. Then train a Soul character for Nour, Karim, and Mama from their 5 best stills (~10 min each; they train in parallel while you keep generating). If training asks for credits or is locked on your plan, skip it — the fallback works fine: attach the reference stills to every keyframe instead.

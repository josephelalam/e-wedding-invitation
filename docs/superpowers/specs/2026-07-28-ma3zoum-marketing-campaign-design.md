# Ma3zoum — Launch Marketing Campaign Design

**Date:** 2026-07-28
**Status:** approved pending owner review
**Occasion:** owner has unlimited Higgsfield generation for one full day (24h window). The campaign is designed so that one day of generation produces a launch identity, a 6-reel story campaign, a test-ready ad bank, and months of evergreen content.

---

## 1. What is being marketed

The managed e-invitation service built in this repo (live at einvite.einvite.workers.dev): trilingual (ar/fr/en) wedding & event e-invitations for the Lebanese market. The owner builds each event; guests receive **personal WhatsApp links** (the link carries their name + honorific); the invitation opens as a **music-backed envelope** into a cinematic template (slides / edges / Horizon deck); couples watch **RSVPs arrive live** on a dashboard.

What is actually sold: a *moment* (mama opening an envelope with the couple's song) and the removal of a *chore* (chasing 300 RSVPs across time zones).

## 2. Brand decision (with collision history — do not re-litigate)

**Brand name: Ma3zoum (معزوم)** — Lebanese dialect for "you're invited."
Chosen by the owner; verified clean 2026-07-28 (no invitation/wedding business found using it; all spelling-variant .coms unregistered). Second-person guest-facing word = the viewer of every ad is addressed as the guest. Plural headline form: "كلن معزومين". Known cultural echo, not a conflict: Saad Ramadan song "معزوم".

Names **rejected for collisions** (verified during this design):
- "EInvite" / "E-invites" — einvite.me is an active competitor, self-styled "#1 Digital Wedding & Event Invitations in Lebanon"; owns the domain + SEO for the term.
- "Aabelkon" / "3a2belkoun" — 3a2belkon.com is an active Lebanese wedding vendor marketplace; identical pronunciation, passing-off risk.
- "Azimeh" / "Dawetna" — clean, but the owner prefers Ma3zoum; kept as fallbacks.

**Sign-off device:** every ad ends "**…وعقبالكن 😉**" — the phrase is cultural commons; used as a sign-off it delivers the emotional hook without building the brand on a competitor's name.

**Taglines:** AR primary "**عزيمة ما بتنتسى**" (an invitation they'll never forget); EN secondary "*The invitation they'll open twice.*"

**Handles & domains (Day-0 actions):**
- Register **ma3zoum.com** + **ma3zoum.me** immediately (~$20 total, insurance while free).
- Handles: **@ma3zoum.lb** — verified available on TikTok and Instagram 2026-07-28. Same handle on both platforms (consistency beats exactness; bare @ma3zoum is taken on TikTok).
- Display name: **Ma3zoum | معزوم**.

**Visual identity** (deliberately matches the product's design system so ads = product): warm ivory paper, engraved gold, deep olive accents; Latin serif "Ma3zoum" + Arabic calligraphy معزوم lockup. Voice: warm, celebratory, winking; premium, never corporate. Captions bilingual AR/EN; French sprinkled, not structural.

## 5. Creative concept — "Nour & Karim are getting married"

One photoreal fictional Lebanese couple created with **Higgsfield Soul 2.0** and locked with **Soul ID** (train once on 3–5 renders per face; the pair then stays consistent across all images, video, and lipsync — a brand-photoshoot level of consistency with zero shoot).

The campaign is their wedding told in **6 reels (15–30s)** where the product is the plot:

| # | Reel | Beat | Product moment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Yes | proposal teaser; "Lebanon, meet Nour & Karim 💍" | none (hook only) |
| 2 | The Send | "we WhatsApp'd our details… 48h later:" | **real screen recording**: envelope opens, music plays |
| 3 | Mama's Reaction | her mother opens *her own* personal link and cries | personal link with Arabic honorific |
| 4 | The Cousin in Montreal | opens at 2am, taps RSVP | dashboard ticks +1 live |
| 5 | The Wedding | cinematic zaffe entrance | "300 guests. 0 printed cards. 1 link each." |
| 6 | Aabelkon 😉 | couple to camera: "وعقبالكن" | CTA: "Your turn? WhatsApp us." |

**Integrity rules (non-negotiable):**
1. Every product shot is a **real screen recording of the live platform** — AI supplies only the humans and emotion; the product is never mocked up.
2. **No fake testimonials.** Nour & Karim are openly a story. Real reviews come from founding couples. UGC-*style* ads dramatize the story, never masquerade as customer reviews.
3. Follow platform AI-content labeling rules (IG/TikTok AI labels) — being upfront is consistent with the trust positioning.
4. Music: never bake licensed commercial tracks into exported files. The `nour-karim` demo event gets a **royalty-free** track (screen recordings capture the envelope-open audio — the existing demo events' owner-uploaded song must not leak into ads). Trending sounds are added natively inside IG/TikTok (licensed there); otherwise royalty-free.

**Live demo funnel trick:** create a real `nour-karim` demo event in production. Link-in-bio opens the actual invitation as a guest — the ad doubles as a hands-on product demo.

## 6. The 24-hour Higgsfield asset list

Higgsfield capabilities in play: Soul 2.0 (photoreal stills), Soul ID (character lock), Cinema Studio (AI Director shot breakdown + per-shot camera moves), lipsync, 30+ video models (Sora 2 / Veo 3.1 / Kling 3.0 …) for take variety. Unlimited day = render ~10 takes per shot and keep the best.

- **Layer 1 — Launch kit (statics, while Soul ID trains):** logo explorations (gold-on-ivory lockup, pick 1); pfp + banner; 5 highlight covers (Demo · Packages · How It Works · Reviews · Contact); 9-post launch grid (3 brand cards, 3 framed product screenshots, 3 couple teasers); pricing carousel; "3 steps" carousel; FAQ carousel.
- **Layer 2 — Story series:** the 6 reels above; 4–8 Cinema Studio shots each + product screen recordings + AR/EN captions.
- **Layer 3 — Ad bank (15–20):** alternate first-3-second hooks for reels 2/3/4 (cost-pain, diaspora, mama-cried, 48h-speed); 3 clearly-fictional UGC-style spots; 5 pain-point statics (print-cost math, group-chat chaos, RSVP spreadsheet horror, time zones, 48h speed); 3 six-second bumpers (envelope → music → معزوم).
- **Layer 4 — B-roll vault (30–60 shots):** zaffe, dabke circle, church/mountain/beach venues, rings, dance floor, candle aisles — future post material, generated while other renders queue.

Generation-day pipeline (detail lives in the implementation plan): morning = Soul ID training + Layer 1; midday = Layer 2 shots; afternoon = Layer 3; evening = Layer 4 + selects. Screen recordings are captured **before** the day starts.

## 7. Channels & publishing

- **Instagram = home base** (Lebanese wedding discovery), **TikTok = reach**, cross-post to **Facebook for mothers** (decision-makers), **WhatsApp Business = conversion floor**.
- Launch week: profile + 9-grid + reel 1 on day one → one reel every 2–3 days → daily stories (polls, this-or-that venues, BTS).
- Post 7–9pm Beirut — catches Lebanon evenings and diaspora daytime (Americas).
- Paid: after 48h organic test, boost the best reel **$5–10/day, Lebanon + Lebanese diaspora, engaged-status targeting, ages 22–38** (~$150 initial).
- Vendors: DM 20 planners/photographers — 15–20% referral cut + one vendor-facing asset ("your couples will thank you").

## 8. Funnel & ops

- Bio: "💌 عزيمة ما بتنتسى · Managed e-invitations · 🎵 opens with your song · 📲 ↓" → link page: live Nour & Karim demo invitation, wa.me (pre-filled text), pricing.
- WhatsApp Business: greeting message, quick replies (AR/EN price card, how-it-works, demo link), 3-package catalog. Reply SLA ≤1h during launch week.
- Booking flow: DM/WA → demo + price card → 50% deposit → details form → live in 48h → post-wedding testimonial + tag ask.
- Measurement: distinct wa.me pre-filled text per asset family (e.g. "Hi! I saw Nour & Karim's invitation 👀") so bookings attribute to hooks; link-page UTMs.

## 9. Risks & mitigations

- **Handle/domain squatting after launch** → register domains + handles Day 0, before anything goes public.
- **AI-people backlash** → integrity rules §5; label per platform norms; pivot to real founding-couple content as it arrives.
- **Zero-follower cold start** → boosted best reel + vendor referrals + founding-couples discount carry the first bookings; organic compounds later.
- **Owner capacity (side business)** → all assets are pre-generated; posting is scheduling work; WhatsApp quick replies cut response effort.

## 10. Out of scope (this campaign)

- Custom product domain / white-labeling (product keeps workers.dev URL for now; ma3zoum.com can redirect or host the demo link page later).
- Self-serve signup, new product features, non-wedding event marketing.
- Paid search / Google Ads.
